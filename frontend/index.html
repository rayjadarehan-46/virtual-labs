Four Probe Resistivity Measurement

Calculate the resistivity of a material using the four-probe method.

Measurement Inputs

Results

Resistance: - Ω
Resistivity: - Ω·m
Conductivity: - S/m
Sheet Resistance: - Ω/sq

Four Probe Method

Formula: ρ = R × 2π × s × t

Where:

  • ρ = Resistivity
  • R = Measured resistance
  • s = Probe spacing
  • t = Sample thickness

The four-probe method eliminates contact resistance effects, providing accurate resistivity measurements.

Laser Beam Divergence

Calculate laser beam divergence and beam size at different distances.

Parameters

Results

Divergence Angle: - degrees
Beam Diameter @ Distance: - mm

Laser Divergence

Formula: θ = 4λ / (πD)

Where: θ = Divergence angle, λ = Wavelength, D = Beam diameter

Laser beam divergence increases with wavelength and decreases with larger beam diameters. Red lasers (650nm) diverge more than infrared (1550nm).

Laser Distance Meter - Time of Flight

Calculate distance using laser pulse time of flight measurement.

Measurement Input

Microseconds (1 μs ≈ 300m distance)

Results

Distance: - m
Distance: - cm

Time of Flight Method

Formula: d = (c × t) / 2

Where: d = Distance, c = Speed of light (3×10⁸ m/s), t = Time of flight

Laser distance meters emit a pulse and measure the time it takes to reflect back. The division by 2 accounts for the round trip.

Wavelength Measurement - Diffraction Grating

Determine wavelength of light using diffraction grating equation.

Parameters

Results

Wavelength: - nm

Grating Equation

Formula: d × sin(θ) = m × λ

Where:

  • d = Grating spacing
  • θ = Diffraction angle
  • m = Order of diffraction (1, 2, 3...)
  • λ = Wavelength

Diffraction gratings split light into different wavelengths. Each order shows maximum intensity at specific angles determined by the grating equation.
